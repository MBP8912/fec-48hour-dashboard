What these figures mean

Not all fundraising

Form 6 notices report qualifying contributions of $1,000 or more received in the final pre-election reporting window. They are not a measure of all daily campaign receipts.

May include noncash items

FEC notice rules also cover certain loans and in-kind contributions. The amounts here are the reported face values of those notices, not a verified cash-only total.

Traceable to the source

Every entry is linked to its original FEC Form 6 contribution page. Amendments and repeated transaction IDs are reconciled to avoid double counting.
