Reading the results

Votes vs. reporting

Candidate vote percentages are the share of ballots counted so far. Reporting progress is labeled as either estimated vote counted or precincts reporting. Those are different measurements.

Counties within a district

Congressional districts may cross county boundaries. County totals must be specific to this race, not total votes from the entire county.

Unofficial / incomplete

A leading candidate has not necessarily won. Official results can change as mail, provisional, and other ballots are counted and certified.
